TECHNICAL CAPABILITY

Patent work begins with understanding the technology.

Our approach connects the inventor’s implementation, technical contribution and alternative embodiments with a clear patent strategy.

Technology-focused patent support

Complex patent matters require careful communication between inventors, engineers, patent professionals and decision-makers. We review the system architecture, process flow, interfaces, operating conditions, alternatives and measurable technical outcomes before defining the drafting or prosecution approach.

How technical review improves the work

  • Identifies the difference between the inventive concept and routine implementation
  • Surfaces missing embodiments, interfaces and operating scenarios
  • Improves continuity between the claims, description and drawings
  • Supports meaningful fallback positions for prosecution
  • Helps distinguish prior-art combinations using the actual technical operation

Inventor discussions and invention harvesting

For research teams and product groups, invention harvesting may involve structured sessions to identify protectable concepts across a product or research programme. We help separate the central concept from implementation choices, determine which alternatives require disclosure and prepare a filing roadmap aligned with development and disclosure plans.

For startups, research institutions and corporations

The level of process should fit the organisation. A startup may require rapid prioritisation before a launch or fundraise; a university may require disclosure development and inventor coordination; and an established company may require portfolio-wide consistency, outside-counsel reporting and jurisdiction-specific review.
