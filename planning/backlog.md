### 1. Inter IKEA Systems v Algemeen Vlaams Belang (C-298/23)
- **Date:** 8 September 2026
- **Jurisdiction:** EU, CJEU (Grand Chamber)
- **Area:** Trade mark
- **Summary:** Freedom of expression under Article 11 of the Charter can in principle be "due cause" for using a sign similar to a reputed mark. Simply invoking it is not enough. National courts must weigh the user's purpose and public-interest contribution against the harm to the proprietor. The case concerned a Belgian party's "IKEA-PLAN" campaign that imitated IKEA's assembly instructions, and using a mark just to borrow its reputation for a political message does not automatically prevail.
- **Why it matters:** This is the leading EU test for parody and political or commentary uses of famous marks. It is useful for Indian brand owners enforcing in the EU and for comparing with Indian parody and disparagement case law.
- **Primary (opened):** CJEU press release, https://curia.europa.eu/site/jcms/p1_1000086739 (judgment PDF: https://courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/inter-ikea-systems-bv-v-algemeen-vlaams-belang-vzw-cjeu-judgment.pdf)
- **Secondary:** https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/9/cjeu-establishes-framework-for-balancing-trademark-rights-and-freedom-of-expression
- **Day:** Thu · **Priority:** High

### 3. EPO Enlarged Board of Appeal G 1/25 (adaptation of the description)
- **Date:** 3 September 2026
- **Jurisdiction:** EPO
- **Area:** Patent
- **Summary:** After claims are amended, the description has to be adapted only where an inconsistency with the amended claims causes non-compliance with an EPC requirement, such as Art 84 clarity. Formal concordance alone is not required.
- **Why it matters:** This changes daily EPO prosecution practice for Indian and US applicants, who can now resist boilerplate description-amendment objections. It pairs naturally with the G 1/24 post (already covered) and G 1/26 (item 16).
- **Primary:** Not opened. Decision PDF as cited by Mondaq: https://link.epo.org/web/case-law-appeals/Communications/G_1_25/Decision_of_the_Enlarged_Board_of_Appeal_of_3_September_2026.pdf

### 60. Satius Holding v Samsung Electronics (Fed. Cir. No. 25-1446, precedential)
- **Date:** 1 Oct 2026
- **Jurisdiction:** US Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (Moore C.J. writing, with Lourie and Hughes JJ), on appeal from the District of Delaware (Magistrate Judge Christopher J. Burke)
- **Area:** Patent / indefiniteness and enablement (US 6,711,385, wireless communication with impedance matching couplers)
- **Summary:** The district court held claims 1, 11 and 18 indefinite because they required something scientifically impossible (transmitting electric signals over the air as claimed).
