Sanath V Madgula

Also known as Sanath M V. Co-founder of PRASA IP, Bengaluru. Advocate and registered Indian patent agent.

At a glance
  • Name: Sanath V Madgula, also written Sanath M V.
  • Role: co-founder and partner, PRASA IP, JP Nagar, Bengaluru, with a US office (PRASA IP LLC).
  • Professional status: Advocate and registered Indian patent agent.
  • Experience: more than 12 years in patent drafting, prosecution and analysis.
  • Education: bachelor's degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering; master's degree in Microelectronics, Newcastle University, United Kingdom; LLB.

Practice

Sanath's practice focuses on patent drafting and prosecution, patentability and prior art searches, freedom to operate studies, invalidity and infringement analysis, patent landscapes, evidence of use analysis, responses to examination reports and office actions, and international portfolio coordination. He supports clients seeking patent protection in India, the United States, Europe and other jurisdictions.

He works closely with inventors, research teams and business leaders to understand technically complex innovations and to build intellectual property strategies that match commercial objectives.

Technology areas

Wireless communication, electronics, semiconductors, artificial intelligence and machine learning, mechanical systems, quantum technologies, blockchain, the Internet of Things, automotive technologies and biomedical technologies.

Before PRASA IP

Before co-founding PRASA IP with Praneetha K Rasbag, Sanath built his patent experience at leading intellectual property law firms in India, working on technically demanding drafting, prosecution strategy, prior art analysis and complex patent matters across sectors.
