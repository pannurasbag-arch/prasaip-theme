FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about protecting intellectual property.

General information only; the appropriate strategy depends on the invention, brand, jurisdiction and commercial objective.

What is the difference between a patent, trademark, design and copyright?

A patent can protect a qualifying invention; a trademark identifies the commercial source of goods or services; a registered design protects eligible visual features of a product; and copyright protects qualifying original expression such as software code, text and artwork.

When should an inventor contact a patent professional?

Ideally, before publicly disclosing, selling or presenting the invention. Early review helps identify disclosure gaps, possible filing routes and whether a prior-art search would be useful before drafting.

Can PRASA IP assist with patent applications outside India?

Yes. We support Indian, convention and PCT filing strategies and coordinate matters involving the United States, Europe and other jurisdictions through appropriate filing routes and foreign counsel where required.

What does a patentability search examine?

A patentability search reviews relevant prior-art documents to assess whether key features of an invention appear new and potentially inventive. The results can guide drafting and filing decisions, but do not guarantee grant.

Does PRASA IP support startups and research institutions?

Yes. Support may include invention harvesting, patent searches, drafting, filing, prosecution, trademark and design protection, portfolio planning, IP training and coordination of international filings.
