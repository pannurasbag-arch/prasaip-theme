Privacy policy

Last reviewed: 26 September 2026

This page explains, at a general level, how information submitted through the PRASA IP website may be handled.

Information you provide

Information may include your name, organisation, contact details and the contents of an enquiry. Please do not submit confidential invention details, identity documents, financial information or sensitive personal data through a general website form unless a secure method has been agreed.

Purposes

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess whether the firm can assist, administer an engagement, maintain security and meet applicable professional or legal requirements.

Analytics and cookies

Where analytics or non-essential cookies are used, appropriate notice and controls will be provided in accordance with applicable requirements.

Retention and rights

Information should be retained only for legitimate professional, legal, security and administrative purposes. Applicable rights and contact procedures depend on the relevant law and the nature of the information.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to PRASA IP through the contact details shown on this website.
