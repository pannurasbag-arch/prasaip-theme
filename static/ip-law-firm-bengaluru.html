How to choose an intellectual property lawyer or patent attorney in Bengaluru

Bengaluru has many IP firms, and they differ in ways that matter to the outcome. Before you engage one, check:

  • Registration. Ask for the patent agent's registration number and check it on the IP India register of patent agents. Patent agents are qualified by examination to practise before the Indian Patent Office.
  • Technical fit. Your drafter should understand the technology well enough to describe alternatives and fallback positions, not only the version you built.
  • Who does the work. Confirm who drafts the specification and who answers examination reports, and whether that person is the one you met.
  • Written scope and fees. A clear engagement letter should set out each stage, the professional fee for it and the official fees payable to the Patent Office or Trade Marks Registry.
  • Deadline control. Patents and trademarks run on statutory deadlines. Ask how deadlines are docketed and how you will be reminded.
  • International reach. If you plan to file in the US, Europe or through the PCT, ask how foreign filings are coordinated and who is responsible in each country.

Typical timelines for an Indian patent

  • Provisional to complete: a provisional application secures your filing date; the complete specification must follow within 12 months.
  • Publication: about 18 months from the priority date, or sooner if early publication is requested.
  • Request for examination: must be filed within 31 months from the priority date. Startups and certain other applicants can request expedited examination.
  • First Examination Report: once issued, the response deadline is 6 months, extendable by 3 months.

Grant time depends on the technology area, the objections raised and whether a hearing is needed, so we give a matter-specific estimate rather than a single figure.

Common questions

When should I consult a patent professional?

Ideally before a public demonstration, publication, sale or disclosure outside an appropriate confidentiality arrangement. Bring a technical description, drawings if available, the dates of any disclosures and a list of intended countries. The possible effect of disclosure depends on the jurisdiction and facts.

Can a Bengaluru firm support US or European patent matters?

Yes. We provide drafting, claim analysis, prosecution support and coordination. Where a jurisdiction requires representation by a locally qualified professional, that professional handles the relevant representation and legal responsibility.

Does a trademark application guarantee registration?

No. The Registry examines the application, and third parties may oppose it. A pre-filing search and carefully prepared application can help identify issues, but the outcome depends on the mark, goods or services, prior rights and evidence.

What happens at the first discussion?

We identify the subject matter, relevant deadlines, jurisdictions and the decision you need to make. Before receiving unpublished technical material, we can agree the engagement scope and a secure transfer method.

Which patent office handles applications from Bengaluru?

The Patent Office branch at Chennai has jurisdiction over applicants from Karnataka. Filing and most examination steps are online, and hearings can be held by video conference.

How much does a patent cost in India?

The total depends on the invention's complexity, the number of claims and pages, the applicant category and the objections raised in examination. We quote each stage separately in writing; official fees are charged at the rates in the IP India schedule.

Is a patent agent the same as an IP lawyer?

Not always. A registered patent agent is qualified to practise before the Indian Patent Office. An advocate can represent clients in courts. At PRASA IP, both founders are registered patent agents and advocates, so they can advise across the Patent Office and the courts.
