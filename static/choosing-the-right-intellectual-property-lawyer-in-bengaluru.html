What an intellectual property lawyer does

The work falls into three parts. First, advice: which of your assets can be protected, which right fits each one and in which countries it is worth filing. Second, registration: drafting, filing and prosecuting applications at the Patent Office and the Trade Marks Registry, and answering objections until the right is granted. Third, protection: watching for conflicting filings, opposing them, sending notices and supporting litigation when someone copies your work.

Good IP advice also shapes your contracts. Employee and consultant agreements, assignments, licences and confidentiality agreements decide who owns the rights in the first place. Many disputes start because these were never signed.

Lawyer, patent agent or trade mark agent?

In India these roles overlap but are not the same. Only a registered patent agent, or an advocate who is also registered, can file and prosecute patent applications for someone else before the Patent Office. Trade mark filings can be made by an advocate or a registered trade mark agent. Court cases need an advocate enrolled with a Bar Council.

For most founders this means one practical question: who will actually draft and prosecute your application, and are they registered to do it? At PRASA IP, the people named on this page do that work themselves.

How a matter runs with us

  1. First call. You tell us what you have built or the brand you want to protect, and where you sell. We first need a confidentiality agreement in place. Please ask us and we will send one.
  2. Assessment and quote. We tell you which rights fit, in which countries, and what the official and professional fees will be for each stage.
  3. Search. For a patent, a prior art search shows whether the invention is likely to be new. For a trade mark, a clearance search shows conflicting marks before you invest in the name.
  4. Drafting and review. We draft the specification or application and go through it with you until it describes your work correctly.
  5. Filing. We file in India and, where needed, start PCT or foreign filings within the 12 month priority period.
  6. Examination. We answer the examination report. For patents in India the reply is due within 6 months, extendable by 3 months.
  7. Grant, registration and upkeep. We track renewals and deadlines so the right does not lapse.

How to choose an intellectual property lawyer

  • Check registration. Patent agents are listed on the IP India register of agents with an IN/PA number. Advocates are enrolled with a State Bar Council.
  • Match the technology. A patent is only as good as its claims. Ask whether the drafter has a technical background in your field.
  • Ask who does the work. Find out who drafts, who answers the examination report and who you will speak to.
  • Get the scope and fees in writing, stage by stage, including foreign filings.
  • Ask about confidentiality and conflicts. Your lawyer should not be acting for a competitor on the same subject matter.

Frequently asked questions

What does an intellectual property lawyer do?

An intellectual property lawyer advises on which rights to secure, drafts and files patent, trade mark, design and copyright applications, answers objections from the registries and acts against infringement. They also draft the agreements that decide who owns the rights.

Do I need a lawyer or a patent agent to file a patent in India?

You can file your own application, but if someone files for you they must be a registered patent agent. Many IP lawyers are also registered patent agents. Court proceedings need an advocate.

How much does an IP lawyer cost in India?

Official fees for a patent application with a request for examination start at ₹5,600 for a startup, and a trade mark application costs ₹4,500 per class for a startup or individual. Professional fees are extra and depend on the complexity of the work and the number of countries.

Can PRASA IP protect my invention or brand outside India?

Yes. We prepare and coordinate PCT, US and European patent filings and Madrid trade mark filings, and work with our US office, PRASA IP LLC, on US matters.

How do I check if a patent agent is registered?

IP India publishes the register of patent agents with each agent's registration number. Ask for the number and check it against the register before you instruct anyone.
