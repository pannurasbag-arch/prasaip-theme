Patent agent or patent attorney? India, the US and Europe

Who may file and prosecute a patent application for someone else depends on a register, not a job title. We explain the rules in India, the United States, the European Patent Office and the UK. We also show how to check a practitioner on the official register.

By Sanath V MadgulaPublished 21 November 20267 minute read

What is the difference between a patent agent and a patent attorney? In India, the Patents Act, 1970 recognises one role for practice before the Patent Office: the registered patent agent. In the United States, both titles exist, and the difference is whether the registered practitioner is also a member of a bar. In Europe, the test is whether the person is on the European Patent Office's list of professional representatives.

The question is topical in India. IP India's Patent Agent Examination 2026 drew 2,402 candidates for Paper I, and 344 qualified for the viva voce stage. Registration for the 2027 examination is open, according to an IP India notice updated on 18 November 2026.

Key points
  • In India, only a registered patent agent may practise as an agent before the Controller, under sections 127 and 129 of the Patents Act, 1970.
  • An advocate who is not a patent agent may still take part in a hearing before the Controller for a party, under section 132(b).
  • In the US, registered practitioners who are lawyers are patent attorneys. Those who are not are patent agents. Both must pass the USPTO registration examination.
  • At the EPO, representation is by professional representatives on the EPO list, mostly European patent attorneys who passed the European qualifying examination.
  • Each office publishes a register you can search before you instruct anyone.

India: the registered patent agent

India's rules sit in Chapter XXI of the Patents Act, 1970. Section 125 requires the Controller to keep a register of patent agents, which may be kept in electronic form. Section 129(1) provides that no person shall "practise, describe or hold himself out as a patent agent" unless registered. Section 129(2) bars companies and other bodies corporate from doing so.

Who may register. Under section 126, the person must be a citizen of India, at least 21 years old, and hold a degree in science, engineering or technology. The person must also pass the qualifying examination, or have served for at least ten years as an examiner or in the Controller's functions. The prescribed fee must be paid.

The examination. Rule 110 of the Patents Rules, 2003 sets a written test and a viva voce. Paper II covers "drafting and interpretation of patent specifications, design specifications and other documents". A candidate needs at least fifty marks in each paper and sixty percent in aggregate. The 2024 amendment to the rules added design law to the syllabus.

What an agent may do. Section 127 entitles a registered agent to practise before the Controller and to prepare all documents and transact all business in proceedings under the Act. In practice this covers drafting, filing, responding to examination reports, oppositions and hearings.

Who else may act. Section 132 preserves two routes. An applicant may draft its own specification and appear before the Controller. An advocate who is not a patent agent may take part in a hearing before the Controller on behalf of a party. The Act's chapter on agents uses only the term patent agent. "Patent attorney" is a common description in India for a person who is both an advocate and a registered agent, not a separate statutory title.

Enforcement is different. Infringement suits and revocation petitions are heard by courts, where advocates appear. A firm that both prosecutes and litigates therefore needs both qualifications. Our guide to the patent filing procedure in India explains where the agent's work fits.

United States: attorney or agent, both registered

Under 35 U.S.C. § 2(b)(2)(D), the USPTO may govern the recognition and conduct of those who represent applicants. Its rules are in 37 C.F.R. Part 11. Section 11.10(a) provides that only registered practitioners, and individuals given limited recognition, may represent others in patent matters.

Section 11.6 draws the line between the two titles. A US citizen who is an attorney may be registered as a patent attorney. A US citizen who is not an attorney may be registered as a patent agent. Both must pass the registration examination unless it is waived, under section 11.7.

The difference matters outside patent prosecution. Under section 11.14(b), registration as a patent agent does not by itself allow practice in trademark matters. USPTO registration also does not admit anyone to a state bar or give a right to appear in court. Foreign applicants usually work through a US registered practitioner. Europe: professional representatives before the EPO

Article 133(1) EPC states that no person shall be compelled to be represented. Under Article 133(2), applicants without residence or principal place of business in a contracting state must be represented. Filing the application is an exception, and the Implementing Regulations allow others. A US or Indian company therefore needs a representative for most steps after filing.

Under Article 134(1), representation may only be undertaken by professional representatives on a list kept by the EPO. Entry requires, among other things, passing the European qualifying examination. Article 134(8) also lets legal practitioners qualified in a contracting state, and with a place of business there, act. This applies only so far as national law entitles them to act in patent matters. The EPO now keeps a searchable database of professional representatives.

At the Unified Patent Court, parties may be represented by lawyers authorised before a court of a member state. European patent attorneys entitled to act before the EPO may also appear if they hold an appropriate qualification, such as the European Patent Litigation Certificate.

United Kingdom: a protected title

In the UK, "patent attorney" is a protected description. Section 276 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 restricts the words patent agent and patent attorney. An individual who is not registered may not carry on business under a name containing them, and breach is an offence. Sections 277 and 278 allow European patent attorneys, and solicitors for the words patent attorney, to use the titles. Section 276 protects the title. It does not reserve filing work before the UK IPO to registered attorneys. The register is kept by IPReg.

How to check a practitioner

  • India: search the electronic register of patent agents on the IP India website by name or registration number.
  • United States: use the USPTO practitioner search to confirm registration and whether the person is listed as an attorney or an agent.
  • Europe: search the EPO database of professional representatives.
  • United Kingdom: search the IPReg register.

Practical steps

  • Applicants should check the register before instructing anyone, and confirm who will sign filings and appear at hearings.
  • Applicants filing in several countries should expect a registered practitioner in each office. One adviser can coordinate, but cannot sign everywhere.
  • Companies expecting litigation should confirm that the team includes advocates or lawyers with rights of audience, not only prosecution practitioners.
  • Candidates for the Indian examination should check eligibility under section 126 first, including the degree requirement and Indian citizenship.

Frequently asked questions

Can an advocate file a patent application in India?

An applicant may file its own application, and an advocate may take part in hearings before the Controller. Practising before the Patent Office for others as a patent agent requires registration under the Patents Act, 1970.

Is a patent agent the same as a patent attorney in India?

The Act recognises registered patent agents. The term patent attorney is used informally for a person who is both an advocate and a registered patent agent.

How do I check if a patent agent is registered?

Use the official register of the relevant office. These are the IP India register of patent agents, the USPTO practitioner search and the EPO database of professional representatives. In the UK, use the IPReg register.
