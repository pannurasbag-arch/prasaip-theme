Criteria set by the owner: free, no spam, strong topic fit. Every site below is a genuine editorial outlet. Its published guidelines were checked on 13 October 2026 and show no fee. Getreach was ruled out because every IP or legal offer there is a paid, unmarked dofollow link.

Ground rules for all ten:

* Each pitch is for a separate, original piece. Do not send one article to two outlets, and do not resubmit text already on prasaip.com.
* The piece has to be worth publishing even without a link. Link to prasaip.com only where the editors allow it, usually in the author bio or as one source citation.
* Write the pieces yourselves. LiveLaw and the IPKat reject text written or rewritten by AI.
* Do not comment on a case the firm acted in. LiveLaw and the IPKat both bar this, and Kluwer requires a disclosure.
* Ten new articles is a lot of writing. Starting with the top three or four is fine.
