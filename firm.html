ABOUT PRASA IP

A technically grounded intellectual-property practice.

PRASA IP supports innovators, businesses, research institutions and counsel across India and internationally in developing, protecting and managing intellectual property.

Our firm

PRASA IP LLP is headquartered in Bengaluru and serves clients across India and internationally. PRASA IP LLC has a US office in Sheridan, Wyoming. The practice combines close senior involvement with disciplined technical and legal analysis. The firm assists with patents, trademarks, copyright, industrial designs, trade secrets, semiconductor layout-designs and related legal requirements.

Our work extends from invention identification, searches and drafting through filing, prosecution, hearings and portfolio coordination. International matters may involve Indian, PCT, US and European routes, with appropriately qualified foreign counsel engaged where required.

Development of the practice

Founding of the practice

PRASA IP launched with a client-focused practice in patent strategy, drafting, filing and prosecution.

Practice expansion

Services expanded across searches, trademarks, designs, copyright, portfolio management, IP training and startup support.

International coordination

The firm developed cross-border drafting and prosecution support for Indian applicants, overseas counsel and multinational teams.

Today

PRASA IP continues to build a technology-focused practice led directly by its founders in Bengaluru, with a US presence in Wyoming.

Our practice across India

Founding partners Praneetha K Rasbag and Sanath V Madgula work with teams that need technical understanding alongside legal analysis. The firm supports patent drafting and prosecution, trademark and design protection, and detailed SEP, claim chart and licensing work.
